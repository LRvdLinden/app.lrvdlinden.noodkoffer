Noodpakket Privacy Policy
Last updated: 12 November 2026

Noodpakket does not collect personal data. No account, no ads, no tracking and no analytics.

Data in the app
Your household, the contents of your kit with expiry dates and your emergency plan are stored on your own device. On iPhone and iPad they sync, when you are signed in to iCloud, through your own private iCloud database between your own devices. The developer has no access to this data. On Android everything is stored in the app's private storage.

Barcode scanning
When you scan a product, the app only looks up the barcode number at Open Food Facts and Open Products Facts. No other data is sent. Product data is available under the Open Database License (ODbL).

Camera
The camera is only used when you scan a barcode or an expiry date yourself. Images are processed on the device and are not stored or sent.

Notifications
Reminders are local notifications scheduled by your device.

Messages from Noodpakket
If you turn on Messages from Noodpakket, you will now and then get a short message from the developer, for example to check your kit before a storm. For this, the server stores a random device number created by the app, the app language and version, and on iPhone and iPad the Apple push token. An Android phone checks for new messages itself every few hours. There is no account, and no name, email address, location or kit data is sent. If you turn it off, the registration is removed right away on iPhone and iPad; an Android phone that stops checking is removed automatically after six months.

Your rights
Delete the app to erase all your data. On iPhone, also remove the app's iCloud data in Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage.
